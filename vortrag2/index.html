Homework for the next session

One page for your CFO

The valuation you saw in class goes to the board next week. You are still in the finance team.

Pick one of its three critical numbers: the peer multiple of 8.6x, the flat FX assumption of 1.05, or the enterprise value of 47.3 million euros.

  • Apply the TRA test to it in writing: traceability, reproducibility, accountability.
  • State whether you would release it, and under what condition.
  • Name what you would need in order to be able to check it. Be specific: a source, a date, a screening rule.

One page is the limit, and that limit is part of the task.

Bring it to the next session. We open with it, and two of you present. What is graded is the reasoning, not the verdict.
